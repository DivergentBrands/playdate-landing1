Questions

Good to know

Is this a sanitizer?+

No. Playdate™ is a cosmetic hand & surface cleanser, not a hand sanitizer or disinfectant. It's formulated to gently cleanse and wipe away dirt from hands, faces, toys, and surfaces.

What's actually in it?+

Three ingredients: purified water, hypochlorous acid (HOCl), and salt. It's alcohol-free, water-based, and dermatologically tested for sensitive skin.

Does it smell like bleach?+

Hypochlorous acid has a very light, faintly clean scent that's naturally part of its chemistry — it's not the harsh, overpowering bleach smell some cleaners have. Most parents describe it as barely-there.

Is it safe for my child's skin?+

It's formulated to be gentle, alcohol-free, hypoallergenic, and dermatologically tested for sensitive skin. As with any new product, we recommend a parent supervise use and always wash with soap and water when it's available — Playdate™ is a gentle extra for when it's not.
