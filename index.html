Questions

Good to know

Is this a sanitizer?+

No. Playdate is a cosmetic hand and surface cleanser, not a hand sanitizer or disinfectant. It is formulated to gently cleanse and wipe away dirt from hands, faces, toys, and surfaces.

What is actually in it?+

Three ingredients: purified water, hypochlorous acid, and salt. It is alcohol free, water based, and dermatologically tested for sensitive skin.

Does it smell like bleach?+

Hypochlorous acid has a very light, faintly clean scent that is naturally part of its chemistry. It is not the harsh, overpowering bleach smell some cleaners have. Most parents describe it as barely there.

How long does it last?+

Hypochlorous acid is freshest when it is made in small, sealed batches, which is exactly how we bottle it. Each bottle is freshness-dated, and because we pre-fill every size, including travel, you are never decanting it into another container where it can lose its gentleness. Use within the date on the bottle for the best experience.

Is it safe for my child's skin?+

It is formulated to be gentle, alcohol free, hypoallergenic, and dermatologically tested for sensitive skin. As with any new product, we recommend a parent supervise use and always wash with soap and water when it is available. Playdate is a gentle extra for when it is not.
